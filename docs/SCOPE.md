# Scope — 846 Autos Demo

**Status: Production-ready**

## Demo includes
- Premium responsive homepage
- Separate Home, About, Services, Vehicles, Contact, Privacy Policy, and Terms of Service routes
- Lounge & Bar and Snooker prioritized in the hero and service hierarchy
- Animated hero with pause/resume controls
- Automatic hero image transitions
- Animated headline/word transitions
- Services presentation
- Vehicle showcase
- Clearing & forwarding presentation
- Car wash presentation
- Lounge/bar presentation
- Snooker presentation
- Visual gallery
- About/company information
- Contact/CTA with verified phone and WhatsApp channels
- Contact form with service selection
- Mobile navigation
- Responsive layouts
- Accessibility states (skip-to-content, Escape-to-close, aria-controls, focus states, reduced-motion)
- Basic SEO metadata (per-route title/description, Open Graph, JSON-LD LocalBusiness, sitemap.xml)
- Performance-conscious image handling (lazy loading, async decoding, preconnect)

## Possible enhancements
- Vehicle detail view
- Gallery filtering
- Advanced scroll choreography
- Page transitions
- Interactive service cards
- Google Maps
- Social links
- PWA-style caching
- Real owner-approved imagery (replacing Unsplash/Pexels demo assets)
- Production domain connection (Cloudflare Workers custom domain)

## Explicitly deferred
Database, API, authentication, admin dashboard, payments, online bookings, customer portal, vehicle checkout, inventory management, AI assistant, CMS.

## Scope principle
If a feature does not materially improve the owner's ability to understand or evaluate the proposed website, prefer leaving it out of the first demo.
